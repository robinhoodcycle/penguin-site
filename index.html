Why the price is in water

Every other memecoin is priced in SOL. This one is priced in an acre-foot of California water.

On ICE Market a token launches against a commodity rather than against SOL or a stablecoin. PENGUIN launched against H2O, which tracks the Nasdaq Veles California Water Index — the benchmark price for water rights traded in California, quoted per acre-foot.

That makes the exchange rate below a real one, not a metaphor. It moves when water moves.

The unit 1 acre-foot 325,851 gallons. Enough to cover an acre of land a foot deep, or supply two California households for a year.

The record

First community token to graduate on ICE Market

A token starts life on a bonding curve. It graduates when enough has been bought to fill that curve, at which point the liquidity moves to a permanent pool and can never be withdrawn — not by the creator, not by the protocol.

Holding it

Holders are paid in water, not dollars

Half of every trading fee goes to the people holding PENGUIN, and it arrives denominated in H2O. You are not paid a share of revenue in a stablecoin; you are paid in the commodity the market is priced against. Payouts run on a schedule and land in your wallet on their own.
